## 5. Evidence vs. Assumptions

### Evidence from Employer Sources

- Xsolla's role description emphasizes frontend frameworks, performance, accessibility, and collaboration.
- Ketryx explicitly identifies API development, database work, testing, and operational reliability as responsibilities.
- Ketryx requires at least three years of full-time industry experience.

### Interpretations and Assumptions

- Frontend development may be a useful direction for demonstrating my visual web-development experience.
- Backend development may align with my experience implementing application logic and database operations.
- A portfolio containing both interface screenshots and technical explanations could demonstrate a broader range of skills.

These interpretations are not employer requirements. They are conclusions drawn from the research.

## 7. Alternatives Considered

**Option A: Frontend-focused portfolio**

Advantages:
- Highlights interface design and accessibility.
- Makes visual demonstrations easy to inspect.

Limitation:
- May not adequately demonstrate backend development experience.

**Option B: Backend-focused portfolio**

Advantages:
- Highlights databases, APIs, and application architecture.
- Supports discussion of technical problem-solving.

Limitation:
- Backend functionality may be difficult to demonstrate visually.

**Option C: Full-stack portfolio**

Advantages:
- Demonstrates both interface and server-side experience.
- Allows different employers to inspect relevant project evidence.

Limitation:
- Requires clear organization to prevent the portfolio from becoming unfocused.

**Proposed decision:** Use a full-stack portfolio structure, with separate explanations of frontend and backend contributions.

This is a proposed direction rather than a completed implementation.
